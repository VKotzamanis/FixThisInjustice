# The next phase: five jobs, in order

> **Read `docs/HANDOFF-2026-09-10.md` first.** It holds the state. This holds the work.
> Steps use `- [ ]`.

The onboarding is finished apart from one designed-but-unbuilt piece. The owner now edits the app's
own words and colours in a tool built for him, and the work shifts from "implement his feedback" to
"take his edits, then build the rest of the app".

---

## Job 1: take his design export and put it in the source

He edits at `https://vkotzamanis.github.io/FixThisInjustice/?design=1` and pastes you a JSON
patch. Everything in it is his decision. Your job is to land it, not to improve it.

- [ ] **Check the patch has an `r10` field.** If it does not, he exported from a cached bundle and
      the edits are incomplete. Tell him to open the page in a private tab and export again. This
      has already cost one round trip.
- [ ] Save the JSON and run `node scripts/design-patch.mjs <file>`. It prints a diff and writes
      nothing. Read the diff.
- [ ] Re-run with `--write`. It rewrites `tokens.css`, `copy.ts`, the skin tables and
      `introSlides.ts` in place, preserving comments and order. It refuses a key that is not
      already there, so it cannot invent one.
- [ ] **Regenerate the catalogue after any copy or R10 change**: `node scripts/alpha-catalogue.mjs`,
      then `--check`, then `node scripts/alpha-walk-pages.mjs`, then `--check`. `catalogue.json`
      pins every string byte for byte, so skipping this turns the suite red.

### Notes are not edits

The patch's `notes` array carries what the tool deliberately would not do for him: promoting a
bullet to a heading, deleting one, moving something. **Implement those by hand.** The applier
prints them and writes nothing, because rewriting a nested array literal means generating source
and a comment above a deleted bullet has nowhere to go.

### When his copy breaks a rule

His words may fail the copy contract: a button over three words, a heading not in Title Case, an em
dash. The panel warns him live, but he can export anyway.

**Do not silently correct him.** Say which rule the string breaks and what it would have to become,
and let him choose. He wrote the words; the rules are ours. A silent fix means he sees his sentence
changed and does not know why.

The exception is mechanical and safe: if `check-title-case.mjs` is the only failure and the fix is
capitalisation alone, make it and say so in the commit.

## Job 2: deploy it, and confirm it rather than assume it

- [ ] Run the gates: `npx tsc -b --force`, `npx eslint ./src ./scripts ./build`,
      `node scripts/check-no-emoji.mjs`, `node scripts/check-title-case.mjs`, both catalogue
      checks, `npm run build`, and the suite.
- [ ] Push, then **wait for both workflows on YOUR commit, not on whichever finished last.** A
      naive wait loop catches the previous commit's runs completing and reports success for work
      that has not built yet. Match on the head SHA.
- [ ] **Verify the deployed bundle contains what you shipped.** Fetch the page, read the asset hash
      out of the HTML, and grep the chunk for a string only your change introduces. Twice in one
      session a change was reported live while the old bundle was still being served.
- [ ] Tell him to **open a private tab or clear site data**. It is a PWA: the service worker holds
      the old bundle and a plain reload does not replace it. He has hit this twice.

## Job 3: the application proper

Stage 1 is the onboarding. Stages 2 to 6 — Today, a training session, after the session, settings
and data, extras — have had one round of feedback and none since. `docs/feedback/walk/` holds a
page per stage.

**Start with the known defects, not with a fresh walkthrough.** Three are already written up and
two of them reach the user.

- [ ] **The programme horizon.** `docs/plans/2026-09-10-15-programme-horizon-design.md` is a 551-line
      design and the largest unbuilt piece in the project. Its Task 5 enumerates the ten `next()`
      routes the wizard fold will break. The owner's verdict: programme length "is NOT something
      that's helpful, it's introducing more bugs than intended".
- [ ] **After "Programme Complete", `closeWeeks` grades every later week as a miss and the
      motivation modal fires on them.** No `completedOn` gate exists in `weekly.ts`, `selectors.ts`
      or `trigger.ts`. Finishing a programme starts a stream of "you missed this week" notifications
      that never stops. This is live.
- [ ] **`LogView`'s grid may accumulate those weeks too.** The design agent inferred this from a
      test title and said so rather than claiming it. Read the code before acting.
- [ ] Only then walk stages 2 to 6 and collect his feedback the way rounds 2 and 3 did.

### Testing on this machine

The owner runs MATLAB in another session. A full `vitest run` gets killed under that memory
pressure, and the kill looks like a failure.

- [ ] Run `npx vitest run --maxWorkers=1`, by directory, in the foreground with an explicit long
      timeout. Directories: `src/domain/ src/content/`, `src/ui/`, then
      `src/app/ src/store/ src/skins/ src/design/ src/test/ src/config/ build/`.
- [ ] **`Test timed out` is not an assertion failure.** Check for `AssertionError` before reporting
      one. Two agents nearly reported a red suite that was a starved machine.
- [ ] Count the files you ran against `find src build -name '*.test.ts*' | wc -l`. Chunking by hand
      once missed `src/config` entirely, and the run looked clean over an untested directory.

## Job 4: the themes, once he supplies the art

`docs/design/2026-09-04-icon-register.csv` holds 26 rows. Almost all are still frames.

Known placeholders in the app today: `comfort-1-starting`, `comfort-2-machines`,
`comfort-3-freeweights`, `equip-1-bodyweight`, `equip-3-home`, `equip-5-full-gym`,
`sprite-subsection-marker`, `reset-cookies-icon`, plus the `tape-*` and `bf-*` rows for the body
step. `ArtworkPlaceholder` renders at six sites in the wizard.

For each asset he sends:

- [ ] Put the file in `public/` under the register row's name.
- [ ] Add the line to the register. **Do not edit rows you did not add** — that file is his record
      of what he commissioned.
- [ ] Replace the frame, keeping the frame's dimensions so nothing reflows.
- [ ] **Check it on all three skins.** Two of this project's worst defects were visible on limelight
      only, at 1.41:1 and 1.04:1 against the lime ground. An icon that reads on near-black can
      disappear on lime.
- [ ] Measure the bundle before and after. The precache is around 1.1 MB; state the delta in KiB
      rather than estimating it.

Two of the three assets he has owed longest are the `sprite-subsection-marker`, which cannot ship as
the real person's likeness that was originally asked for, and the Home Screen app icon, which still
shows the previous app's `W.CONSOLE` logo.

**Design Mode Task 3 would let him judge placement before committing the art.** It is not built. If
he wants to try assets in place first, build it before this job rather than after.

## Job 5: rest games, and radio

Both are new. Neither is designed. **Design before building.**

### The games are already promised in shipped copy

`src/content/introSlides.ts` tells every user: *"there are small games to play during the rest
between hard sets."* The feature does not exist. The owner confirmed the sequence deliberately —
build the games once the intro is done — so this is now due rather than a defect, but the app is
currently making a claim it does not keep.

Design questions to answer before writing code:

- [ ] Rest periods are already timed: `src/domain/training/restTimer.ts` holds the cited rest
      intervals, three of them, with the evidence for each. A game has to fit inside a real rest
      interval and **must not encourage him to overrun it**. The rest length is prescribed and
      sourced; a game that makes people sit longer changes a training variable.
- [ ] It must be interruptible. The set is the point; the game is not.
- [ ] It must work offline. This is a PWA with no backend beyond the reminders Worker.
- [ ] Decide whether a score persists. Anything stored goes through `AppState` and the schema, and
      a new stored field is a migration.

### Radio: the CSP blocks it today, and that is the first thing to settle

The owner mentioned a station aggregator, "kasetofono.r" or similar — **verify the actual service
and URL with him before building anything.** Do not guess at a domain.

The blocker is concrete:

```
media-src 'self' blob:
connect-src   pinned to the deployed reminders Worker at build time (build/cspPlugin.ts)
```

Third-party audio is refused outright. Playing a stream means widening `media-src` and probably
`connect-src` to that host, and `scripts/check-dist-csp.sh` gates the built CSP in CI.

- [ ] **Treat the CSP widening as a decision, not a step.** This app holds health data. There is a
      precedent in `tokens.css`: fonts are self-hosted through @fontsource specifically to keep
      `font-src 'self'`, recorded as "security constraint 19". Widening `media-src` to a third party
      reverses that posture for a convenience feature. Get his explicit agreement, and record it
      with the reasoning.
- [ ] Check what the service's terms allow. Embedding or rebroadcasting a stream is not always
      permitted, and this repository's README makes licence claims.
- [ ] Decide what happens offline and when the stream dies mid-session. A training app that hangs
      on a dead socket during a set is worse than one with no radio.

## What not to do

- **Do not silently rewrite his words.** Job 1 says it once; it is the instruction most likely to be
  ignored under time pressure.
- **Do not widen the CSP without asking**, and do not add a runtime dependency to avoid asking.
- **Do not report a deploy as live from a workflow's status.** Verify the bundle.
- **Do not add a second copy of a rule, a constant or a status table.** Nine defects across rounds 2
  and 3 belonged to no brief, and most were one value transcribed into a second place. Derive it, or
  make one test read both sides from the same source.
