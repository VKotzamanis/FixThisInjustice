Camp, maximal, affectionate. Hot pink #FF5FCB and acid #D6FF3B on aubergine #16041F; Bungee for the shouting, Work Sans for everything you actually read. Emoji are inline Twemoji SVG, not typed characters, so the same glyph renders identically on Android and iPhone.

The rule that keeps it safe: the joke is always about the app, never about the user. Numbers and units are never skinned — 140 lb, 63.50 kg, 61.2 kg, 2340 kcal print exactly as computed, and the honest sentence sits directly under the funny one. Plan-altering semantics are unchanged: on hiatus holds the cursor, not today satan advances it and asks why.
