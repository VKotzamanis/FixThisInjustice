Design H — departures board

The plan as a flight schedule. Every session is a service with a time, a label, a gate and a status: scheduled sessions read ON TIME, today's reads BOARDING, a completed one DEPARTED, a skipped one CANCELLED, a paused plan HELD. The week review is the arrivals summary and the rest timer is a gate countdown.

This one keeps the clinical register. No slang, no emoji, no exhortation — every term below is a real airport term used in its real sense, and none of them softens what the app is doing. CANCEL SERVICE still advances the cursor; HOLD SCHEDULE still freezes it. Status is never carried by colour alone: every row also prints its word.

Assets. Type: Space Mono and DM Mono, Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Everything else — flap cells, the continuous seam, the hinge sweep — is pure CSS. This is the only direction of the four with no external graphic at all: no image, no SVG, no data URI.

Two measurement notes. The flap seam is a 1 px line of --board drawn over each character at 50 % height. The contrast checker measures declared colours, not rendered pixels, so that hairline is not sampled; it is a standard split-flap artefact and covers roughly 2 % of a 38 px glyph. And the honest one: with prefers-reduced-motion: reduce set, the flip does not run, and what is left is an amber monospace table. Whether the typography and the status vocabulary still carry the direction without motion is the test this file is for.
