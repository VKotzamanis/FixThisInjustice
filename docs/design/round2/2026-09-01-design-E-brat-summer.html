Design E — brat summer

One flat lime field (#8ACE00), one stretched narrow sans (Archivo at wdth 62), lowercase everything, and one original mascot. No cards, no radii, no shadows, no second hue — hierarchy is size alone. Black on lime is 10.91:1; white on lime is 1.92:1 and appears nowhere. The resample blur is on the 76 px session label only: no quantity is ever blurred.

The mascot is an original pixel drawing generated for this project — a round pink blob in black sunglasses with a black bob. It is not, and must not become, any existing character.
