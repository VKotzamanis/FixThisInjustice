## Design specificity verdict
Authored, not category-interchangeable. The lime ground, black lit signs, pink stickers, the 62 % Archivo display cut and the owner's pixel character could belong to no other fitness app. The failures are in the shell and in the session screen's craft, not in the identity: the frame was built desktop-first (sticky bar + in-flow tab strip + footer) and adapted down, so on a phone the brand chrome takes 85 px, navigation scrolls away, and live state (rest, toasts) lands in the wrong place.

## What is working
- The palette rules are measured and enforced (`tokens.css:154-168`, `limeContrast.test.ts`); where a component follows them, legibility is excellent (ink on lime 10.91:1, sticker 7.75:1).
- Atlas: black owned cards vs dashed "?" locked cards read instantly and carry rarity without colour alone (`iphone13-atlas.png`).
- The rest sign as a black panel with lime digits and a pink arc, and the rest-over flip at 2 Hz, is the right signal; it is in the wrong place, not the wrong form.
- Her sprite on Today and the week stamp give the app a face; the intro's single-sentence slides at 16 px are calm and legible.

## Persona red flags
- **Between-sets lifter (project persona: one thumb, chalked hand, 60-180 s rest, phone on a bench):** cannot see the countdown while at the set rows (defect 2); taps Log and dismisses a toast instead (1); must scroll 900 px past notices to reach set 1 (5); switching to Log to check last week's load loses the typed set values (audit F02); Android back closes the app mid-session (audit F01).
- **Casey (distracted mobile):** primary actions are at the top or below the fold, never in the thumb zone; nav off screen (3); Start below the fold (6).
- **Sam (accessibility):** palette active row 1:1 and marquee 1.92:1 (8); 20 px checkbox and 10.4 px labels (5); tick labels 6 px (9).
- **Jordan (first-timer):** wizard is clean, but the header title reads "w..." and browser-default radios look out of place (12); the CRT vignette is on (14).

## Direction: refine within limelight
Keep everything the owner approved: lime ground, ink type, pink stickers and offset shadows, the 62 % display cut, black lit signs, her sprite, the pixel icon bank, the owner's words. Change the frame and the placement of live state:
- **Two black bands frame the lime.** A one-line black header at the top (safe-area padded) and a fixed black tab bar at the bottom; the lime column between them is the only scrolling surface.
- **Live state is a sign, pinned where the thumb is.** The rest countdown becomes a compact black bar docked above the tab bar; toasts become small signs under the header that never take taps outside their own controls.
- **One sticker per screen is the primary action.** Today's Start and Train's active Log are pink stickers at 56 px; everything else is a plain lime control.
- **Plain controls, themed surfaces.** Every input, radio, checkbox, range and focus ring is drawn from the tokens; no browser blue or white.
- **One heading system.** Display cut for view and hero headings without numerals, the uppercase title for numeral headings, with the shadow offset scaled to the type size.
Rejected: a black (dark-mode) ground for the gym: the owner pinned limelight and lime-with-ink measures 10.91:1, which already serves bright light; a dim-gym case is better served by larger type than by inversion. Rejected: keeping the top tab strip and making it sticky: 7 labels still need 854 px, and the top edge is the hardest reach for one thumb.
