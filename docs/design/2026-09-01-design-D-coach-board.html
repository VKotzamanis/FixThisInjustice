Design D — Coach board

Near-black ground, one signal colour, numerals at display size. Every screen has exactly one filled action; plan-altering controls stay visible as outlined 48 px buttons rather than being hidden behind a menu. Plan progress is a stacked bar of completed / skipped / remaining, so a skip is never silently absorbed into a percentage. The lb–kg toggle sits at the top of the training screens, not in settings. No generated imagery.
