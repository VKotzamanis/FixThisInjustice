Design B — Clinical dashboard

Light ground, black-on-white body text, one encoding per question. Chosen for glanceability under bright overhead gym lighting, where a dark screen becomes a mirror. Four distinct data forms carry the weekly data: a hero signed delta, a categorical seven-day compliance strip, two line sparklines, and bullet bars with a target band for the protein low–high range. No generated imagery is used; every mark is inline SVG or CSS.
