Design I · limelight

Round three. Direction E (brat summer) and direction F (stan twitter) merged into one skin, skin id limelight. From E: the flat lime field #8ACE00, lowercase, Archivo at wdth 62, hierarchy by size alone, the mascot. From F: the marquee, the quote-tweet, the MOTHER stamp and its sparkle fan, the drop-shadowed display face.

The palette rule, computed and absolute: black on lime is 10.91:1; pink on lime is 1.41:1 and the darkest pink that still reads as pink is 4.25:1, so pink is never type on lime at any size. Pink is a fill, an outline and an offset shadow. Pink is type in exactly one place: inside a black panel, at 7.75:1. Every icon silhouette is carried by its outline, never its fill, which is why the same drawings work on both grounds.

No emoji characters anywhere in this file. All sixteen icons are original pixel art generated for this project and palette-snapped to the mascot’s own colours. No arithmetic on any screen: reasons live behind a why? disclosure, one sentence each.

The sixteen icons that replaced every emoji

Generated as one 4 × 4 sheet with agy / Nano Banana, sliced by connected component, downscaled with a premultiplied area average and snapped to seven opaque colours plus alpha. The palette is the mascot’s own, which is what makes the icons and the illustrations one drawn world. 180 to 492 bytes each. Shown here at their true 32 × 32.

Five carry a derived panel variant in which the black outline is remapped to lime, because a black outline is invisible on a black panel. Outline lime on black is 10.91:1; fills stay pink at 7.75:1.

The rules every entry was checked against. Buttons at most 3 words, hero lines at most 8, advice lines at most 12. Everything lowercase except three shouted strings, and the list is closed at three. No em-dashes. No emoji. No arithmetic: a percentage, a rounding step, a delta formula and a unit conversion are justifications and live behind why?; a load, a rep count and a rest duration are instructions and stay on screen. Numbers, units and control semantics are never skinned. The joke is about the app or the week, never about the user, which is why plan_progress reads 1 skipped and not 1 flop: a week can be a flop era, one session someone missed for a reason on file cannot.

Assets and licences. Type: Archivo (variable, wdth,wght), Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1, one family at two widths carrying both the 76 px display and the 12.5 px footnote. Bungee, which direction F used for display, is gone: it has no width axis and no usable small-size range, and this skin needed one family that could do both jobs. Icons: sixteen originals plus five derived panel variants, generated for this project with Gemini / Nano Banana through agy, sliced, downscaled and palette-snapped here. Own work, no third-party rights, no attribution obligation. Illustrations: four originals (mascot lifting, being crowned, flopped, resting) plus round two’s mascot, same provenance, 2.6 to 4.0 kB each after palette-snapping to 256 × 256. Pixelarticons (MIT, 1036 icons, 24 × 24) and Kenney’s 1-Bit Pack (CC0 1.0, 1078 tiles, 16 × 16) were evaluated and not used: neither holds any of the camp pictograms, Pixelarticons rescales 24 to 32 at a non-integer ratio, and the Kenney pack ships tilesheets with no per-icon names. No third-party character, no real person, no brand, no song lyric, no album text and no artist name appears anywhere in this file, and there is not one emoji character in it.
