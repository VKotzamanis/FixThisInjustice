Where the code disagreed with you

Each of these has a file and a line behind it. Any of them can be overridden — they are findings, not refusals.

C1.04.4

Storing both unit systems would break the thing you asked for

You wanted a units toggle that needs no recalculation. You already have one: every quantity is stored canonically and formatted at render, so switching units costs nothing today. Storing both would add two sources of truth that can disagree, and round-trip drift at every display boundary.

src/domain/types.ts · LoggedSet.enteredUnit already records what you typed

C1.09.5

The activity levels are not arbitrary, so your own condition rules out the slider

You said “let’s revise this whole thing if it’s arbitrary”. It is not. They are three FAO/WHO/UNU 2004 PAL bands, and the file already records rejecting the five-point gym ladder you’d recognise, because its interior points are arithmetic interpolations with no source.

Your actual complaint stands, though: “sedentary in Houston” and “sedentary in New York” are different lives. That is a labelling failure, not a banding one, and the labels get rewritten with the examples you asked for.

src/domain/nutrition.ts:150 · FAO Table 5.3 p.38

C1.07.16

Neither hormone-therapy bullet can ship, and the evidence says why

No study validates any RMR equation in a population on gender-affirming hormone therapy. And no evidence supports a six-month threshold: van Velzen 2020 found 20.2% of transmen showed no measurable body-composition change even at 24 months, so a rule keyed to elapsed time misroutes one user in five.

The better answer was already in your code. Supplying a body-fat percentage moves the app onto Cunningham (1991), which has no sex term at all.

DOI 10.1530/EJE-20-0609 · src/domain/nutrition.ts:109

C1.13.6

The protein form you named doesn’t matter

“Aim for isolate / concentrate / peptide” — no clinically meaningful difference in hypertrophy between the three. What differs is lactose content and price. What does matter: total daily intake, about 0.4 g/kg per meal, and third-party testing, since 14.8% of 634 supplements across 13 countries carried undeclared anabolic steroids.

DOI 10.3390/nu11092047 · DOI 10.1055/s-2004-819955

C1.13.4

Milk and sugar blunting caffeine is folklore

The dose and the timing hold — 3 to 6 mg/kg, about 60 minutes before, and coffee works as well as a capsule. The milk-and-sugar clause has no evidence behind it. Also: your dose should be computed from body mass the way creatine already is, and capped, because 6 mg/kg at 90 kg exceeds EFSA’s safe single dose.

DOI 10.1186/s12970-020-00383-4 · DOI 10.2903/j.efsa.2015.4102
