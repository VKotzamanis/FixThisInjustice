Setup, step 3: body fat

DoScroll down the same screen. Open "why?", then pick the tape option.

covers setup.body

  1. advice.bodyFatOptional

    Optional. With it, RMR uses the Cunningham equation.

  2. disclosure.why

    why?

    This string keeps its plain wording on every skin: CASE ALONE, OR THE LIMELIGHT WORD ALREADY. A word change here is out of scope; a layout or behaviour change is not.

  3. why.bodyFatOptional

    With a body-fat value: Cunningham for RMR, protein per kg of fat-free mass. Without one: Mifflin-St Jeor, protein per kg of body mass.

  4. label.bodyFatNone

    Not measured

  5. label.bodyFatKnown

  6. label.bodyFatTape

    Estimate from tape measurements

  7. quantity.bodyFat

  11. quantity.abdomenII

    Abdomen II

  12. quantity.hip

    Hip

  13. advice.tapeNeedMale

    Enter neck and abdomen II girths.

  14. advice.tapeNeedFemale

    Enter neck, abdomen I and hip girths.

  15. advice.tapeOutOfDomain

    Girths outside the equation's domain. No estimate is shown.

  16. advice.bodyFatEstimate

    21.9 % body fat, ± 3.52 percentage points.

  17. why.bodyFatEstimate

    The figure after ± is the standard error of the US Navy estimate against hydrostatic weighing. It is larger than most changes worth chasing, so track change over time rather than the absolute number.

You seeOne screen, seven numbered questions, Yes and No on each.

covers setup.readiness, readiness.screen

  1. step.readiness

    Readiness

  2. advice.notMedicalAdvice

    This is not medical advice.

  3. advice.readinessAnyYes

    Answer yes to any question: consult a physician before training.

  4. why.readiness

    These seven questions are this app's wording of the seven PAR-Q+ screening domains. No answer is stored, only the screening date and whether any was yes. A yes is not a diagnosis and this app is not a clearance instrument.

  5. readiness.q1

    Has a doctor diagnosed you with a heart condition?

  6. readiness.q2

    Do you get chest pain at rest or during physical activity?

  7. readiness.q3

    In the last 12 months, have you lost consciousness or lost balance from dizziness?

  8. readiness.q3Note

    Answer no if the dizziness came only from over-breathing during exercise.

  9. readiness.q4

    Has a doctor diagnosed you with another chronic condition?

  10. readiness.q5

    Do you take prescribed medication for a chronic condition?

  11. readiness.q6

    Do you have a bone, joint or soft tissue problem that activity could worsen?

  12. readiness.q6Note

    Answer no if the problem no longer limits your physical activity.

  13. readiness.q7

    Has a doctor told you to exercise only under medical supervision?

  14. label.yes

    Yes

  15. label.no

    No
