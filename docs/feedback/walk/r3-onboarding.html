What is deliberately not done, so you are not hunting for it

  • Three assets are yours. The subsection sprite, the reset icon on the review screen, and the Home Screen app icon — which is still the previous app's W.CONSOLE logo. All three ship as placeholder frames.
  • Ten strings are Title Case but still questions or sentences rather than noun phrases, including "How Should I Refer to You?" and five headings on Today that carry your own full stops. Whether to reword them is a judgement call, so they were flagged rather than changed.
  • The programme step still asks for a length. Deriving it from the target date, and the just-in-time split you asked for, is written up and deliberately held back — it rewrites the scheduler while reminders, the calendar export and the Plan tab all read the forward plan.
  • Sliders on the goal, availability and programme steps. You asked for "a slider and a total revision". Step 4 now genuinely has three working sliders; the other three steps were revised without becoming sliders. Tell me if you meant it literally.
  • Nobody has read the 74 cited papers against the claims the app makes from them. Every DOI was checked against the registry and every one resolves with matching metadata. That proves the citation is real, not that it supports the sentence next to it.
